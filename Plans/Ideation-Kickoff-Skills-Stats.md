## The question

What are stats *for* in the 8.1 game — and what levels them?

## What the live game has (facts, not commitments)

- **14 stats on one shared 1.08 curve** (Strength, Defense, Vitality, Focus, etc.), leveled today by: direct PP purchase (stats panel), the **neural implant** (siphons 25% of PP income into one target stat, trains offline), **holodeck programs** (train two stats while *de-leveling* a third, NGU-style real cost), and the **combat sim rig** (STR/DEF while standing at it).
- A 2026-07-31 audit added an honest description to every stat row and found **Dexterity and Perception have no gameplay consumers at all** — their rows literally say "Dormant subsystem — no effect yet." They need a real effect or removal; this session should rule on them.
- The **old Phase F plan** (RS-style skill web: use-XP from doing, direct purchase retires, each skill emits one multiplier) is **NOT assumed under 8.1** — it's an input to steal from, not a default.

## The 8.1 constraints that bound the answer

- **The player is a cyborg**: gear = atoms half, implants/firmware = bits half. The handoff's lean: "stats likely hang off gear + implants."
- **Gear-as-level is Locked** (M2), and §9 says "**gear sets your stats**" for combat — so the session must answer: is there an independent stat ladder at all, or are stats a *derived* readout of gear + implants?
- **Three growth surfaces must have distinct jobs**: gear tier (permanent, atoms), Ichor assignment (per-run allocation — combat pace, gathering speed, crafting throughput, scanning), and stats. If a stat and an Ichor channel and a gear bonus all "make combat faster," the game has three names for one lever. What does each uniquely own?
- **Atoms stay, bits reset.** Which half are stats? Live stats never reset. If stats become implant firmware (bits), do they persist as computer-granted permanents, or does some training layer reset at offload?
- **The ship test**: every mechanic must gather bits, move atoms, or trade time — and nothing grows free (training that drains a third stat is the house style).
- **North star: be the most like NGU** (its stat training with real opportunity cost), with space×time as our addition (the holodeck/trainers are *places* — presence could matter).

## Walk out with rulings on

1. Do the 14 stats survive, shrink, or become derived-from-gear+implants?
2. What levels them — use-XP, trainers/holodeck, gear, computer levels, some mix? Do the implant + holodeck survive as systems?
3. Dexterity & Perception: real effect, repurpose, or removal.
4. Stats vs Ichor vs gear: one sentence each on what only that surface does.
5. Atoms or bits: what (if anything) about stats resets at offload.
6. Whether any of it locks — say "lock it" explicitly, per the log's ground rules.
