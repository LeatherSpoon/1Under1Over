## The question

Which multipliers survive 8.1, which fold into the Computer's levels, and which retire — and does "every system emits one visible multiplier" stay law?

## The live chain (facts — recomputed every frame, all honest/visible)

- **PP rate** = base engine × ascension amplifier (Archive shop) × challenge rewards × factory ring bonus, on top of the **tripartite board** (rate / capacity / power legs — the rebirth-scale investment layer; power = sink-effectiveness everywhere).
- **Combat damage** = gear/stats × boss trophies × challenge rewards × ascension combat amplifier.
- **Drones** = modifiers × ascension drone amplifier × compute gate. **Gather speed** = ascension harvest amplifier × modifiers. **Compute gate** multiplies ladder/extractors/processing/holodeck/factory when units are assigned.
- Also feeding in: tech tree node effects, augmentations, mastery tracks, trade-off **modifiers** (max 2, each boosts one thing by taxing another), TimeWarp temporary boosts.
- The design language that built this: NGU's pillar — *every system emits one visible multiplier into a chain the player can read.*

## The 8.1 constraints that bound the answer

- **PP is KEEP** (the spend currency, run-scoped). **Tripartite is PARK** — "rebirth-scale investment likely folds into computer levels. Do not extend." **Trials/challenges are PARK.** The chapter integer demotes at M2. So several of today's chain links sit on parked systems — the session must say where their multipliers go.
- **New sources are coming**: the Computer's permanent levels (the biggest ratchet), banked Data, Ichor assignment (per-run throughput on chosen channels), gear tiers (the level itself), blueprints (counters, not multipliers?).
- **The contrast law**: atoms stay, bits reset. Which multipliers are permanent (computer-side) vs run-scoped (Ichor-side)? The live game's "permanent-multiplier convention" (recompute from persisted state every frame) survives either way — this is a design question, not a wiring one.
- **Sinks and sources (§8)**: nothing grows free. Overclock/heat and the power grid are on the table — they'd make some multipliers *conditional*, not flat.
- **The ship test** and **the RNG law** (guaranteed floors, lucky tops) apply to any new multiplier source.
- **North star: NGU** — its number-go-up joy comes from a *legible* chain. Whatever survives should stay readable in one screen.
